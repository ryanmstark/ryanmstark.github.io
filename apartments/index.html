Rent control: what to look for
  • In SF, units built on or before June 13, 1979 may have rent control (capped annual increases). The best bets are flats and units in older 2+ unit buildings, like 339 Lyon below.
  • Most condos and single-family homes rented on or after Jan 1, 1996 have eviction protection but not rent control. So a by-owner condo is often a nicer unit, but the rent can go up freely at renewal.
  • Newer construction (e.g. the 2019 Dogpatch building) isn't rent controlled.
  • To confirm a unit's status, ask the landlord for the year built, or call the Rent Board at 415-252-4600. SF rental laws ↗
Questions to ask every landlord
  • Is the parking spot assigned to this unit, and does it fit a compact sedan (a Mazda 3 sedan is about 15 ft long)? Tandem or shared?
  • Is parking included, or what is the monthly cost?
  • Year built, and is the unit covered by rent control?
  • Does the bedroom have an exterior window?
  • Any required monthly fees beyond rent (utilities, trash, amenity fees)?
